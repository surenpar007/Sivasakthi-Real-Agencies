Before we list or buy any land

Eight papers. Every time.

Land is only as good as its documents. We check all eight and share copies with you before the first site visit.

  • Patta and chittaOwner name matches the seller
  • Adangal / A-registerLand classification and use
  • FMB sketchSurvey boundaries match the stones
  • Encumbrance certificate30 years, no loans or disputes
  • Parent documentsUnbroken chain of sale deeds
  • Layout approvalDTCP and RERA numbers verified online
  • Road accessPublic road, width on record
  • Water and soilGroundwater level and soil type
