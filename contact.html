Questions buyers ask

Before you buy land.

What does DTCP approved mean?

The layout has been approved by the Directorate of Town and Country Planning, Tamil Nadu. Roads, open space and plot sizes follow the rules, and banks give loans on approved plots more easily.

Which documents should I check before buying land?

Patta and chitta, Adangal or A-register, FMB sketch, a 30-year encumbrance certificate, the chain of parent sale deeds, and the layout approval number. We share copies of all of these before your site visit.

Can I get a bank loan for a plot?

Most banks give plot loans on DTCP or RERA approved plots with a clear title. Use the EMI calculator on the land page to plan, and we can help you with the paperwork.

Do you help people sell their land?

Yes. We visit the land, give a fair valuation based on recent registrations nearby, check your documents, and bring you serious buyers until registration is done.

Can I see the land before paying anything?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us and we will fix a site visit. You see the land and the documents before any booking amount.
